Overview & Profile Insights

Free4talk-Pro-v27 Tools adds essential profile metrics to Free4Talk so you always know who you're speaking with before hopping into conversation.

WHAT IT SHOWS

Understand anyone's conversation history in a glance:

  • Languages & Levels: Most-used spoken languages and English proficiency.
  • Favourite Topics: Themes and topics they talk about most often.
  • Top Connections: The specific people they interact with most.
  • Activity Stats: Exact numbers of rooms created, joined, or muted in.

WHERE IT APPEARS

Seamlessly embedded right where you naturally click:

  • Instant Info Button: A subtle info badge on every person's card in the room. Click for a quick popover summary.
  • Native Profile Window: Deeply integrated right inside Free4Talk's own user profile dialog.
  • Full Tracker Link: One-click shortcut to view complete profile records on the free4talk.me tracker.

Privacy by Design

Zero tracking. Zero analytics. Zero ads. Free4talk-Pro-v27 Tools never captures or listens to your audio. It operates solely on free4talk.com, and only interacts with youtube.com when you explicitly choose to link a music tab.

Local Storage Only

Saved room chats and direct message logs remain encrypted strictly on your physical machine and are never uploaded to any remote server.

Direct Spotify Tokenization

Spotify login connects you directly to Spotify's official servers. Your tokens stay in your browser; passwords are never seen or stored.

Anonymous Skip Hashes

SponsorBlock lookups query truncated video ID hashes anonymously—SponsorBlock never receives the URL or title of what you are listening to.
